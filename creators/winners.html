Winner process

Fair. Transparent. Verified.

Every Gaviom Creator sweepstakes uses the same rigorous winner selection process — whether it's a creator-hosted campaign or a Gaviom founding prize.

How winners are selected

A three-step process designed for integrity and auditability.

Entry pool locked

At the scheduled draw time, the entry pool is frozen. All paid entries and valid AMOE mail-in entries are included. No new entries accepted after lock.

Secure random selection

Gaviom uses cryptographically secure random number generation to select the winner from the verified pool. The process is logged and auditable.

Winner verification

The selected entrant is verified for eligibility (age, residency, one entry per person rules). If disqualified, an alternate is drawn.

Winner announcement

Public, documented, and confirmed — for creators and participants alike.
