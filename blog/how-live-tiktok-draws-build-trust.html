How Live TikTok Draws Build Trust in Online Sweepstakes

June 11, 2026 · 6 min read · By Gaviom Inc.

Why Gaviom streams random selection on TikTok, what watchers should expect, and how public draws differ from backroom winner picks.

Trust in online giveaways hinges on one question: Can anyone verify the winner was chosen fairly? Live draws on TikTok answer that in public.

What happens on draw night

Gaviom founding draws stream Sunday at 8pm ET on TikTok. Entrants watch random selection in real time. Recordings stay available for audit and for players who missed the live stream.

Read the full player journey on how Gaviom works and the dedicated post on live TikTok draws.

Why transparency matters commercially

Capped pools publish odds before entries close. Live selection connects those odds to an outcome witnesses can see. That pairing is rare in social-media giveaways, and it is why experienced entrants filter for operators who stream, not just post winner screenshots.

After the draw

Winners complete verification steps described in what happens when you win. Losers keep confirmation emails as proof of entry for their records.
