What Is Gaviom+ Membership? Bundles, Entries, and How It Works

June 10, 2026 · 7 min read · By Gaviom Inc.

A clear guide to Gaviom+ monthly membership, ticket bundles, eligible sweepstakes pools, and how it differs from buying single prize entries.

Gaviom+ is a monthly membership that credits ticket bundles across eligible sweepstakes, built for players who enter regularly, not for one-off prize shopping.

Membership vs single-prize checkout

Buying tickets on individual sweepstakes targets one capped pool with known odds. Gaviom+ spreads entries across the month's eligible promotions, which Gaviom announces before billing per Official Rules.

What you receive each month

  • Automatic ticket credits on activation
  • Flexibility to allocate entries across eligible pools
  • Option to purchase additional tickets at member checkout
  • Same AMOE rights, free mail-in entry still applies per sweepstakes

Who membership fits

Deal hunters entering multiple founding draws benefit from bundle pricing. Casual entrants may prefer single-prize pre-orders. Compare on the membership page before joining.
