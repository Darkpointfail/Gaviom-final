How Benefits Brokers Differentiate With Engagement Perks

June 4, 2026 · 7 min read · By Gaviom Inc.

Brokers win RFPs with stories that go beyond medical and dental. Here is how to position culture-building benefits in executive conversations.

Every broker deck shows the same medical networks. Differentiation happens when you can answer: What does this employer do for culture that candidates will actually mention in interviews?

The engagement perk pitch

Frame engagement benefits as retention infrastructure, not fluff. Tie them to hiring velocity, referral quality, and Glassdoor themes around appreciation, metrics CFOs and CHROs already debate.

Gaviom for Business gives brokers a packaged story: private enrollment, managed compliance, live recognition events, and reporting HR can show leadership. Review the platform overview.

Objections you will hear, and answers

"We already do spot bonuses." Bonuses are individual; shared programs create company-wide moments.

"HR is at capacity." Managed operators handle rules, draws, and fulfillment, HR approves, does not operate.

"Is this legal?" Yes, when structured as US sweepstakes with AMOE and published rules, same framework as consumer promotions, scoped to employees.

Bundle placement in proposals

List engagement perks adjacent to voluntary benefits and wellness, not buried in "other." Include a one-page appendix CHROs can forward to their CEO without rewriting your copy.
