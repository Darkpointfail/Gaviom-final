Gaviom pre-sale lets you reserve entries before founding draws, while caps are known and odds are still calculable. Here is what checkout actually purchases.

What you are buying

Tickets are entries into a specific capped sweepstakes, for example the founding MSC cruise or iPhone promotion, not a generic wallet credit. Each prize page lists ARV, specs, and maximum entries.

You are not buying the prize itself. You are buying chances in a random drawing governed by Official Rules.

Why pre-order before caps fill

Published odds use the cap denominator. As entries accumulate, your share of the pool changes, but the cap keeps expectations honest versus unlimited viral contests.

Bundle pricing on checkout and Gaviom+ can lower per-entry cost for regular players. See entry bundles and odds.

Free entry still applies

Pre-ordering is optional. Mail-in AMOE enters the same pool. Paid and free paths must coexist lawfully, Gaviom documents both before pre-sale opens.
