How Gaviom Odds Work

May 27, 2026 · 6 min read · By Gaviom Inc.

Gaviom publishes capped-pool odds on every founding sweepstakes. See where odds appear, how bundles change your chance, and why AMOE stays equal.

Gaviom odds are simple division: your entries divided by the published cap for that sweepstakes. Every founding promotion displays the cap and your updated chance before you pay—no hidden denominators.

Capped pools make the math honest

Each Gaviom founding sweepstakes sets a maximum entry count in the Official Rules. If the cap is 6,000, one entry equals a 1-in-6,000 baseline chance. Five entries equal 5-in-6,000 (1 in 1,200) because you hold five distinct records in the same pool.

Checkout on pages like the iPhone sweepstakes shows this relationship when you change bundle size—you see the cap and your odds update in real time.

Where odds appear on Gaviom

  • Prize pages — entry cap and promotion ID before you buy
  • Checkout — bundle selector with odds impact per quantity
  • Official Rules — authoritative cap, deadlines, and draw schedule
  • Live draw — public selection on TikTok after the pool closes

For bundle pricing mechanics separate from display logic, read entry bundles and odds explained. That article covers linear improvement; this one covers where Gaviom publishes the numbers.

Free entry stays in the same pool

US law requires that paid and free paths feed one random drawing unless rules clearly disclose a separate promotion. Gaviom's free entry by mail instructions specify handwriting fields, sweepstakes ID, and mailing address so AMOE postcards enter the same pool as checkout entries.

One valid postcard = one entry record with the same odds math as one paid ticket, not a lesser side drawing.

After the pool closes

When entries hit the cap or the deadline passes, no new entries are accepted. Gaviom runs the scheduled live draw, verifies the winner against eligibility rules, and begins fulfillment or cash alternative coordination described in what happens when you win.

Reserved prize value before entries opened is part of why displayed odds matter—you are evaluating a documented promotion, not an open-ended promise.

Frequently asked questions

Where does Gaviom show odds?

On each prize page and at checkout when you select entry bundles. The display updates based on the published cap and how many entries you hold.

Do free mail-in entries have the same odds?

Free AMOE entries join the same random pool as paid entries. One valid postcard equals one entry record unless rules state otherwise.

What happens when a pool fills?

Entries close at the published cap or deadline in the Official Rules, then Gaviom runs the live draw on the scheduled date.

Does buying more entries guarantee a win?

No. More entries improve your proportional chance in a fixed pool but never guarantee selection. Set a personal budget.

How is this different from social media giveaways?

Gaviom caps entries and publishes math upfront. Viral posts often hide total entry volume, making real odds unknowable.
