AMOE · Alternative Method of Entry

Enter free. Same odds as paid entries.

US sweepstakes law requires a free way to enter when tickets are sold. AMOE (Alternate Method of Entry) is that path — no purchase, no payment, one free entry per sweepstakes per person.

What is AMOE?

When a company sells sweepstakes entries, federal and state law requires an equally accessible free alternate method of entry. On Gaviom, your online AMOE submission is combined with paid entries in the same random draw pool — same odds, same prize, same live TikTok draw.

Questions about free entry

Does my free entry really have the same odds?

Yes. All valid entries — paid, bundled, membership, and AMOE — are combined into a single random draw pool for that sweepstakes.

Can I submit more than one free entry?

No. Limit one (1) free AMOE entry per person per sweepstakes per promotion period. Duplicate submissions are discarded.

Will I receive confirmation?

Yes. You receive a confirmation email immediately after your entry is recorded. Keep it for your records.

Can I still enter by mail?

Yes. Mail-in AMOE remains available as an alternate method — see Official Rules §5 for postcard instructions.

Who can I contact for help?

Email amoe@gaviom.com with your confirmation reference and sweepstakes ID.
